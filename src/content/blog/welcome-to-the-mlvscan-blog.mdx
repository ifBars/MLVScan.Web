MLVScan has grown beyond a loader plugin. There's the shared scanner, the browser
scanner, developer tools, and ways to bring scan reports into a release workflow.
Release notes cover individual changes, but they don't always leave much room to
explain why a change matters or what led to it.

That's what I want this blog to be for: a place to talk through updates, explain
the decisions behind them, and share ideas while there's still room for feedback.

## Why a separate blog?

Documentation is where you go to learn how something works today. Release notes
tell you what changed in a particular version. Security advisories explain
specific malicious packages and false-positive cases.

The blog gives the longer development updates a home. A scanner change might
need a bit more explanation than a changelog bullet, especially when it affects
why a mod gets flagged or how a report should be read.

## What to expect

- **Updates:** changes across the loader, scanner, and developer tools, with links to releases and documentation.
- **Engineering:** how detection works, what causes false positives, and the tradeoffs behind changes.
- **Looking ahead:** upcoming ideas and features, including planned opt-in telemetry once the design is ready to share.
- **Practical examples:** ways mod authors can use scanning and reports in their own release process.

I'll distinguish what's available from what's still being considered. An idea
post isn't a release announcement or a promised delivery date. For installation
and API details, the [documentation](/docs) remains the place to start, and
security reports will stay in the [advisories section](/advisories).
