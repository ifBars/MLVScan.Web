This is a place to share what is happening with MLVScan: changes to the library,
the thinking behind new features, and ideas that are still taking shape.

## What to expect

- **Updates:** changes across the scanner and its libraries, with links to releases and documentation.
- **Engineering:** a closer look at how MLVScan works and the tradeoffs behind it.
- **Looking ahead:** upcoming ideas, shared early enough for feedback to matter.

Security advisories will stay in the [advisories section](/advisories). The blog
gives development updates a home of their own.

## Have something you want to see?

Join the conversation on [Discord](https://discord.gg/UD4K4chKak), or open an
[issue on GitHub](https://github.com/ifBars/MLVScan/issues).

You can also follow the [RSS feed](/blog/feed.xml) to catch new posts in your reader.
